NABH & WHO Quality Compliance Case Study

Healthcare Patient Safety & Clinical Incident Intelligence Platform

An AI-powered clinical risk governance system combining Deterministic Scikit-learn Machine Learning, Sublinear TF-IDF, Cosine Similarity RAG Precedent Search, and Google Gemini Generative AI for automated incident triage.

1. Executive Summary & Operational Challenge

Multi-specialty hospital networks generate hundreds of clinical incident reports weekly—ranging from bedside falls and medication dosage errors to autoclave sterilization deviations and ventilator alarms.

  • Manual Triage Bottlenecks: Unstructured narrative reports take 24–72 hours to classify manually.
  • Subjective Severity Scoring: Varying departmental risk thresholds lead to under-reporting of high-consequence near-misses.
  • Regulatory Exposure: Failure to document sentinel events breaches NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals) 5th Edition standards and WHO Global Patient Safety Goals.

2. The 6-Phase Processing Pipeline

  1. Phase 1: NLP Preprocessing & Hazard Lexicons: Whitespace normalization, medical de-elongation, domain clinical hazard lexicon injection across 6 vectors, and Sublinear TF-IDF (1,2) n-gram vectorization.
  2. Phase 2: Deterministic Machine Learning: Balanced Multiclass Logistic Regression (C=1.5) for Category classification and Lexicon-augmented Logistic Regression (C=2.0) for Severity tagging, augmented with deterministic safety guardrails.
  3. Phase 3: RAG Precedent Retrieval & Salience Summarizer: Cosine similarity over historical clinical incident embeddings retrieving Top-3 historical case precedents.
  4. Phase 4: Healthcare CAPA Engine: Standardized NABH/WHO Corrective and Preventive Actions mapping and Composite Operational Risk Score (0–100) computation.
  5. Phase 5: Google Gemini GenAI Synthesis: Dual Root Cause Analysis (Technical vs. Organizational), regulatory accreditation impact, and 2-sentence executive briefs.
  6. Phase 6: Enterprise REST Gateway & GUI: High-performance FastAPI endpoints serving an enterprise clinical intelligence dashboard.

3. Benchmark Incident Test Cases

  • [H001] Patient Safety (Medium): "Patient fall during bed-to-wheelchair transfer due to improper transfer assistance"
  • [H002] Medication Safety (High): "Incorrect medication dosage administered: 10mg IV bolus given instead of 5mg order"
  • [H003] Infection Control (High): "Suspected infection following sterilization deviation in surgical autoclave batch"
  • [H004] Medical Equipment (Critical): "Ventilator malfunction during patient treatment with critical pressure sensor failure in ICU"
  • [H005] Clinical Operations (Medium): "Delay in diagnostic laboratory result processing exceeding critical troponin turnaround time"
  • [H006] Healthcare Compliance (Low): "Incomplete patient identification documentation observed during wristband verification"

4. Regulatory Frameworks

  • NABH 5th Edition: Care of Patients (COP.14), Management of Medication (MOM.5), Hospital Infection Control (HIC.3), Facility Management & Safety (FMS.4).
  • WHO Global Patient Safety Action Plan 2021-2030: Goal 1 (Zero Harm in Care), Goal 3 (Medication Safety), Goal 6 (Fall Reduction).
